One card component, five surfaces. Pick by role, not by looks: `glass` for programme content on wine, `light` for the one fact you want to shout, `dashed` for a secondary offer, `prize` for the giveaway, `plain` for neutral panels.

```jsx
<Card surface="glass" filmstrip hoverLift>…</Card>
<Card surface="light"><Eyebrow tone="onLight">Minden program</Eyebrow>…</Card>
```

At most one `light` and one `prize` card per screen. Cards carry no drop shadow — they separate by value and a gold hairline.
